Our Story

Two Decades.
One Taproom.

Meander Brewing Co. has been a dream of the two owners, Mike Branam and Paul Bushell, for almost two decades.

From countless awards at beer festivals to late nights in each other's home bars, the two have had a clear goal: create some of the best craft beer around. They built the recipes on kitchen scales, stress-tested them on friends, and kept the ones that earned a second pour.

When the time finally came to open the doors, Buford was the natural home. It's where their families had rooted, where the local supply chain is short enough to know every grain and every hop by name, and where a neighborhood taproom could actually mean something. Meander opened in 2024 with the same philosophy that got them there: use the freshest ingredients, respect the styles, and keep pouring what you'd want to drink.

The result is a range wide enough for the craft beer diehard and the casual light-beer drinker to sit at the same table and each be happy with what's in front of them. A pilsner and a tropical double IPA. A cream ale and a farmhouse saison. A root beer for the kids. The name says it: wander through the list.

The Founders

Two Friends. Twenty Years.

Mike Branam
Co-Founder

Michael has been homebrewing for more than 30 years, guided by the same curiosity, problem-solving, and attention to detail that shaped his career in technology and product development. During his career in telecommunications, he was named as an inventor on 21 U.S. patents, primarily involving digital media, telecommunications, network services, and technologies for managing and delivering content. That inventive spirit carries naturally into brewing. From developing new technology to developing a new beer, Michael has always enjoyed figuring out how things work—and how they can work better. Today, that same experimental mindset goes into creating and refining Meander’s beers.

Paul Bushell
Co-Founder

Paul brings a different, but complementary, background. His professional career has centered on financial services, retirement plans, executive benefits, and relationship management. Building relationships and taking care of people have always been central to his work. Those same skills now help shape the welcoming, community-focused brewery he and Michael envisioned.

What We Stand For

Built on Four Things

Locally Sourced

We utilize the wonderful local ingredients available to us to keep the beer as fresh as it can be.

Community First

We aim to be a staple of Buford. Trivia nights, fundraisers, local partnerships — we want to be more than a taproom.

Kid & Dog Friendly

Games to keep the family entertained, an outdoor beer garden for relaxing game, and the big games on the outdoor TV. Well behaved furry friends are welcome inside and out. Try out our adirondack chairs in the beer garden.

Giving Back

Regular fundraisers for local causes. Our patio doubles as a venue for neighbors who need a hand.
