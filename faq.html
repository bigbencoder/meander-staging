Good to Know

Frequently Asked

Dogs, kids, food, parking, private events — the things folks ask us most, all in one place.

PARKING
We have limited parking in front of the brewery but there is a free 3 story parking deck just across the street
ARE CHILDREN WELCOME?
Yes, Meander Brewing is a family-friendly brewery. For everyone's safety and out of respect for all customers, we ask you to keep your children with you at all times.
DO YOU WELCOME DOGS?
Yes, your furry family members are welcome. We just ask they remain on a leash when they come along with you to the brewery. We have water bowls for them to stay hydrated while you do so with the brews.
DO YOU FILL GROWLERS?
Absolutely! Take us with you on your next visit! We also sell 32oz branded Crowlers, but you can also bring in your own if you have one to fill!
DO YOU HAVE OR OFFER FOOD?
While we do not offer food at Meander Brewing, we have teamed up with Italian Pie just a block away! They deliver directly to the brewery, and you can find their menus on our tables. https://italianpizzapie.com/menu
